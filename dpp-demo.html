When is a merge required? Under EU 2023/1542, when two battery products are assembled into a single battery system (e.g. dual-pack configuration, remanufactured assembly, or module consolidation), the resulting system requires its own Battery Passport with a new unique identifier and consolidated attribute values from both source products.

Platform Guide & DPP Explained

Everything you need to understand Digital Product Passports, what the final product looks like, and how to use this platform

What is a Digital Product Passport?

A Digital Product Passport (DPP) is not a document. It is a structured digital data record permanently linked to a physical product throughout its entire lifecycle — from manufacturing through use, repair, and end-of-life recycling.

It was introduced by the EU Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR — EU 2024/1781) as the EU's primary tool for making products traceable, transparent, and circular. The first legally binding DPP is the Battery Passport, mandated by EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542, mandatory from 18 February 2027.

Think of it this way: every product that enters the EU market must carry a digital identity that anyone — a recycler, a regulator, a consumer — can access by scanning a QR code. That identity contains verified data about what the product is made of, how it was produced, how to maintain it, and how to dispose of it responsibly.

The 5 components of a complete DPP
1
Data Record
All mandatory attributes populated and validated — materials, carbon footprint, supply chain, performance, conformity
2
Unique Identifier
A QR code or NFC tag physically on the product linking to the data record. Mandatory under Art. 77
3
Access Endpoint
A URL or API where the DPP data is readable. Must be publicly accessible — no login required for basic information
4
EU Registry
From Feb 2027, every Battery Passport must be registered in the EU central registry. The registry stores the link, not the data
5
Maintenance Process
The DPP must be updated throughout the product lifecycle — when ownership changes, state of health changes, or repairs occur

The QR Code — Mandatory, Not Optional
Battery Passport ID
BAT-NMC811-001

The QR code is the physical-digital bridge of a DPP. Without it, the digital record exists but cannot be accessed from the physical product — and the DPP is therefore non-compliant.

FormatQR code (preferred) or NFC tag. Data matrix also accepted. Must follow ISO/IEC 15459 for the unique identifier format

Attribute Engine
Planned — Sprint 3

The Attribute Engine is the governance layer that makes DPP categories and attributes fully dynamic. Today, attributes are statically defined in the backend and require a developer deployment to change. The Attribute Engine will allow administrators to create, update, version, and retire attributes entirely from within the platform — without touching code.

Dynamic attribute creation — define new attributes with name, description, unit, data type, validation rules, and regulatory reference through a form interface. No backend deployment required.
Versioning and lifecycle management — every attribute change is versioned. Previous versions are retained for historical DPP records. Changes go through a review → approve → publish workflow before becoming active.
AI-assisted generation — feed a new regulatory text (e.g. a delegated act PDF) and the Attribute Lifecycle Manager agent will propose a complete set of new attribute definitions, ready for review.
Validation rules — set allowed data types, value ranges, mandatory/optional flags, and cross-attribute dependencies. Attributes that fail validation are flagged before a DPP is marked compliant.
Import / export — export the full attribute catalog as JSON or Excel. Import pre-defined attribute sets from external sources (e.g. industry consortia or EU standard bodies).
Estimated availability: Sprint 3 — Q3 2026
Audit Log
Planned — Sprint 3

The Audit Log is the immutable, timestamped record of every action taken on the platform — every data change, every compliance status update, every agent action, every export, and every approval decision. It is the core traceability mechanism that regulators and auditors will rely on to verify that your DPP data is trustworthy and has not been altered without authorisation.

Complete event history — every write operation on the platform is logged: who made the change, what was changed, the previous value, the new value, and the exact timestamp. Records are immutable once written.
Filtered views — filter the log by product, user, date range, action type (data edit, approval, export, agent run), or attribute category. Useful for targeted investigation during an audit.
Agent action traceability — every action taken by an AI agent is logged separately with the agent name, the trigger, the steps executed, the approval decision, and the approver. Full accountability for automated actions.
Export for regulatory submission — export a filtered or full audit log as PDF or CSV for inclusion in a market surveillance response, internal audit, or regulatory filing.
Tamper-evident design — log entries are write-once. No user, including administrators, can delete or modify a log entry. Deletion of DPP data is itself logged as an event.
Estimated availability: Sprint 3 — Q3 2026
Export Center
Planned — Sprint 3

The Export Center is the dedicated hub for managing all DPP data exports across the platform. Today, exports are triggered individually per product from the Interoperability page. The Export Center will allow batch exports, scheduled recurring exports, export history tracking, and configurable format templates — all in one place.

Batch export — select multiple products at once and export their DPPs in a chosen format (AAS, JSON-LD, CSV, PDF). Today you can only export one product at a time.
Scheduled exports — configure recurring exports (e.g. every Monday, export all products as AAS Submodel JSON to a shared folder or API endpoint). Useful for keeping BaSyx registries and external systems in sync automatically.
Export history — a full log of every export: who triggered it, when, which products, which format, and whether it succeeded. Useful for audit evidence that data was shared with a regulator or OEM partner at a specific point in time.
Format templates — save custom export configurations (e.g. "Regulator package: NMC batteries, PDF format, include audit trail") and reuse them with one click instead of configuring each export from scratch.
Estimated availability: Sprint 3 — Q3 2026
